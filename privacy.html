PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: August 2026

Overview — Spritext is an iMessage sticker extension. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Stickers you use are sent as part of your iMessage conversations through Apple's iMessage infrastructure, not through any Spritext server.

Data Collection — We do not collect analytics, device identifiers, usage data, or any personally identifiable information from users of the App.

Suggestion Form — If you choose to submit a sticker idea through our suggestion form, any information you provide (such as an email address or description) is used only to review and consider your suggestion, and is not sold or shared with third parties.

Third Parties — Spritext does not use third-party advertising or tracking services.

Contact — Questions about this policy can be sent to spritext.gmail.com.

Changes — This policy may be updated occasionally. Changes will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
